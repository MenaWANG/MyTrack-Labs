MyPathoTrack is a personal health information organiser published by Dr Mena Wang (PhD). The app stores information entered by the user, including household profile details, biomarker results, custom markers, notes, preferred ranges and reminder settings, locally on the user’s Windows device.

MyPathoTrack does not require an account and does not transmit health information to the developer. The app does not include advertising, analytics, session recording or cloud synchronisation. The developer cannot access, sell or share information stored in the app.

Users control their information and may update or delete it within the app. Users may also create JSON backups, CSV exports and privacy-safe diagnostic files. JSON backups and CSV exports contain personal health information, are not encrypted by MyPathoTrack, and should be stored and shared securely. Diagnostic exports exclude household profiles, health results, notes and backup contents.

Microsoft may process Store installation, licensing and purchase information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. MyPathoTrack does not send personal health records to Microsoft.

This policy may be updated when the app’s data handling changes. Questions about privacy may be sent to: [MyTrackLabs@gmail.com](mailto:MyTrackLabs@gmail.com).
