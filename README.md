# MyTrack Labs

**Helping you remember what matters to you.**

Apps for keeping track of what matters in everyday life, from the foods you eat to your pathology results.

[Visit MyTrack Labs](https://menawang.github.io/MyTrack-Labs/)

## Our apps

- **MyPlateTrack** - a food journal for exploring food variety, choosing your Boost foods and planning your next grocery shop. Android: in development.
- **MyPathoTrack** - keep pathology results together, follow changes over time and remember planned tests. [Available for Windows](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n6l9zjl2kzq); Android is planned.
